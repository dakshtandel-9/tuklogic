## 1. The Work
The Assignor created the website and software known as "tuklogic-website" (repository `dakshtandel-9/tuklogic`), including its source code, scripts, route handlers, configuration, design, text, images, animations, AI-generated media created by the Assignor, and documentation (the "Work"). The Work excludes the Third-Party Materials in Section 4.

## 2. Assignment
In return for the project fee, paid in three parts (25% before work began, 25% at mid-project, and 50% on delivery of the licensing and ownership handover), the Assignor assigns to the Assignee, worldwide and forever, all right, title and interest in the Work, including all copyright, trademark, design rights and other intellectual property rights, and the right to sue for past infringement. The Assignee may use, modify, sell, license and relicense the Work without restriction and without crediting the Assignor.

This assignment takes effect when the Assignee has paid the final 50% instalment. To the extent the law does not allow assignment of any right (such as moral rights), the Assignor waives it, or if it cannot be waived, grants the Assignee an exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free license to use it.

## 3. Assignor's promises
The Assignor states that:
1. The Assignor created the Work, or has the right to assign it, apart from the Third-Party Materials.
2. The Assignor has not sold or licensed the Work to anyone else.
3. To the Assignor's knowledge, the Work does not infringe anyone else's rights.
4. The Assignor has listed all Third-Party Materials known to them in `legal/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`.

## 4. Third-Party Materials
Open-source packages, fonts, music and any other material owned by someone else are **not** assigned. They stay under their own licenses, listed in `legal/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`. The Assignor will help the Assignee replace any such item the Assignee cannot license, at no extra cost during the maintenance period in Section 6.

## 5. Handover
Within 7 days of signing, the Assignor will hand over the items in `legal/HANDOFF-CHECKLIST.md`: the repository, hosting accounts, domain, storage, and all credentials, and will keep no copies of credentials. The Assignor may keep a copy of the code only for portfolio use if the Assignee agrees in writing.

## 6. Maintenance
The Work was built over about 4.5 months, ending on the date above. The Assignor will provide 12 months of maintenance from the date above (bug fixes and hosting or deployment support). Maintenance does not affect ownership: the Assignee owns the Work from the moment the assignment takes effect.

## 7. Liability
The Work is provided "as is". Except for the promises in Section 3, the Assignor's total liability is limited to the fees paid.

## 8. General
This agreement is the whole agreement on this subject. Changes must be in writing and signed. It is governed by the laws of India, and the courts of Bangalore, Karnataka have jurisdiction. This is a template. Have a lawyer review it before signing.
